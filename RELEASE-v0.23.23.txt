Malay TTS Bot v0.23.23 - 10-Key Round-Robin + /ask TTS Fix

Gemini API key expansion
- Expands the Gemini API key round-robin from 5 to 10 configured slots.
- Supports GEMINI_API_KEY plus GEMINI_API_KEY_2 through GEMINI_API_KEY_10.
- Empty slots are skipped, slot 10 can be selected as the starting slot, and secret-safe logs continue to show slot numbers only.
- Credential-auth failures still disable only the bad slot until /restarttts or a full process restart.
- Quota/rate-limit and model/project permission failures keep their existing non-rotation behavior.
- Editing .env still requires a full bot process restart to load added or changed keys.

/ask Gemini 3.1 TTS reliability fix
- Fixes /ask repeatedly falling through to Google because dedicated Gemini 3.1 TTS was capped by the normal 1600ms exact-TTS fallback window.
- /ask continues to skip conversational Live, but dedicated Gemini 3.1 TTS now gets the configured 4000ms first-audio timeout before Google fallback.
- Normal Live-first chat keeps exactFirstAudioMs at 1600ms, preserving its existing latency behavior.
- The overall first-audio budget remains 7000ms and the Google reserve remains 1200ms.

Regression and compatibility
- Adds full 1 -> 10 -> 1 round-robin regression coverage and slot-10 start/empty-slot coverage.
- Adds a regression guard proving normal chat remains 1600ms while /ask receives the 4000ms exact-TTS window.
- Normal provider order remains Gemini 3.1 Flash Live -> Gemini 2.5 Native Audio Live -> Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS -> Google Malay TTS.
- /ask speech remains Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS -> Google Malay TTS and is never routed through conversational Live.
- Voice pool, queue ownership, privacy opt-out, STOP TTS, speaker labels, and exact read-aloud behavior are unchanged.

Upgrade
Preserve .env and data\guilds.json. Do not copy old runtime caches, logs, locks, node_modules, or config/settings.json over the new release. Extract the clean package to C:\Malay-TTS-Bot, restore only .env and data\guilds.json, then run setup-clean.cmd.
